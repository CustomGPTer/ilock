What a locker cannot do

iLock is an extra lock for the times your phone is already unlocked and in someone else's hand. It is not a substitute for your phone's own screen lock, and it does not encrypt anything. A person who knows your phone's screen lock, or who has the skill and time to work on the phone, may be able to get past any app locker. Do not rely on iLock alone to protect information whose loss would cause serious harm.
